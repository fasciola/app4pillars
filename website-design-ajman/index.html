Frequently Asked Questions

Website design in Ajman: common questions

Do you design websites for businesses based in Ajman?

Yes. Four Pillars Web Design is a brand of Business Lab, which has an office in Ajman on Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Street, alongside our Dubai office in Business Bay. We work with Ajman mainland and free zone companies on website design, e-commerce and SEO-ready builds.

Can I meet the team in person in Ajman?

Yes. Business Lab's Ajman office at Amber Gem Tower is available for in-person meetings by appointment, which is useful for scoping a project, reviewing designs or discussing an Ajman Free Zone company's website requirements face to face.

How much does website design cost for an Ajman business?

Cost depends on the number of pages, custom functionality, e-commerce requirements and the level of visual design. We scope every Ajman project around the business, rather than a fixed template package.

Will an Ajman free zone company website be SEO-ready?

Yes. Every website we build has a crawlable, server-rendered structure, clean metadata, canonical URLs, structured data and sitemap support, so it can be indexed properly whether your company is set up in an Ajman free zone or on the mainland.

Do you only work with Ajman businesses?

No. We design websites for businesses across Ajman, Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the rest of the UAE. The Ajman page is focused on the needs and search intent of businesses operating in and around Ajman.
